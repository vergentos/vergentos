### Introduction

If you're running a WooCommerce store and using Sage 50 for accounting, you know the pain: duplicate data entry, stock discrepancies, and hours wasted reconciling orders with invoices.

Sage 50 WooCommerce integration solves this by creating a real-time bridge between your online store and accounting software. Here's everything you need to know.

### What is Sage 50 Integration?

Sage 50 integration connects your e-commerce platform directly to your accounting system via API. This enables:

- **Automatic order sync** - Orders flow from WooCommerce to Sage as invoices
- **Real-time stock levels** - Sage stock counts display on your website
- **Customer data sync** - New customers created in both systems
- **Payment reconciliation** - Payments matched automatically

### The Problem with Manual Processes

Without integration, your team manually:

1. Exports orders from WooCommerce
2. Re-enters them into Sage 50
3. Updates stock levels in both systems
4. Reconciles payments at month-end

This creates errors, wastes time, and delays financial reporting. For businesses processing 100+ orders weekly, it's unsustainable.

- **Sage 50 Professional or above** (not Sage 50 Accounts)
- **Sage Data Objects (SDO)** installed
- **WooCommerce store** on quality hosting
- **Windows server** for the sync service (or we host for you)
