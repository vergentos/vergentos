### Introduction

Sparks Transport is a well-established Bristol haulage company with a modern fleet including electric vehicles. Despite their industry-leading sustainability credentials, their online presence didn't reflect who they'd become.

This is the story of how we transformed their digital presence and achieved 340% organic traffic growth in six months.

### The Challenge

When Sparks Transport approached us, they faced several problems:

#### Outdated Website

Their existing site was built years ago and looked it. Slow loading, not mobile-friendly, and featuring imagery of trucks they'd long since replaced.

#### Poor Search Visibility

Despite being a major Southwest logistics provider, they weren't ranking for key terms like "Bristol haulage" or "Southwest logistics company."

#### No Lead Generation

The website had no clear calls-to-action, no quote request system, and no way to capture potential customer enquiries.

#### Recruitment Struggles

With driver shortages industry-wide, they needed a careers portal to attract talent—but had nowhere to showcase their modern fleet and company culture.

### Our Approach

#### 1. Discovery and Strategy

We spent time understanding Sparks Transport's business: their ideal customers, competitive advantages, and growth goals. Key findings:

- Their electric truck investment was a major differentiator
- Commercial clients valued reliability and sustainability credentials
- Recruitment was as important as customer acquisition

#### 2. Professional Photography

Before touching the website, we organised a professional photoshoot featuring:

- Their new electric truck fleet
- Drivers and warehouse team
- Loading operations and logistics facilities

Authentic imagery transforms credibility. Stock photos of generic trucks wouldn't cut it.

#### 3. Website Design and Build

We designed a bespoke website focused on:

**Clear Service Presentation**
- Dedicated pages for each service type
- Coverage area maps
- Fleet specifications and capabilities

**Lead Generation**
- Quote request forms with service-specific fields
- Click-to-call on mobile
- Live chat integration

**Careers Portal**
- Current vacancies with online applications
- Company culture and benefits information
- Driver testimonials and day-in-the-life content

**Technical Excellence**
- Sub-2-second load times
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Accessibility compliance

#### 4. SEO Strategy

We implemented a comprehensive local SEO strategy:

**Technical SEO**
- Site speed optimisation
- Schema markup for local business
- XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration

**On-Page Optimisation**
- Keyword-targeted service pages
- Location-specific landing pages
- Optimised meta titles and descriptions

**Content Strategy**
- Industry news and updates
- Service area guides
- Sustainability and fleet information

**Local SEO**
- Google Business Profile optimisation
- Citation building across directories
- Review generation strategy

#### 5. Sales Collateral

Beyond the website, we designed:

- Company brochure for sales team
- Service-specific one-pagers
- Vehicle livery concepts

### The Results

Six months after launch:

#### Traffic Growth

- **340% increase** in organic search traffic
- **520% increase** in mobile traffic
- **45% reduction** in bounce rate

#### Search Rankings

- **Page 1** for "Bristol haulage"
- **Page 1** for "Southwest logistics company"
- **Page 1** for "haulage company near me" (Bristol area)

#### Lead Generation

- **45+ qualified leads** through quote request forms
- **28% conversion rate** on careers applications
- **60% increase** in phone enquiries

#### Business Impact

- Two major new commercial contracts attributed to website
- Improved driver recruitment pipeline
- Sales team equipped with professional collateral

### Key Takeaways

#### 1. Invest in Real Photography

The photoshoot cost was a fraction of the project but delivered outsized impact. Authentic imagery builds trust that stock photos never can.

#### 2. Local SEO Compounds

Consistent local SEO effort builds momentum. Rankings improved month-on-month as Google recognised the site's authority for Bristol logistics terms.

#### 3. Design for Conversion

A beautiful website means nothing without clear paths to action. Every page should guide visitors toward contact, quote, or application.

#### 4. Mobile Matters for B2B

Even commercial decision-makers research on phones. Mobile experience directly impacted enquiry quality and volume.

### Conclusion

Sparks Transport's digital transformation demonstrates what's possible when strategy, design, and technical execution align.

Their investment in a professional website, photography, and SEO has delivered measurable commercial returns—and positioned them as the modern, sustainable logistics partner their brand deserves.
